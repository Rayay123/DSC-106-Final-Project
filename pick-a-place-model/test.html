Where is the gap largest?

Color shows how much warmer SSP5-8.5 is than SSP1-2.6 by 2091–2100. Scroll over the map, or drag a box, to zoom in. Once zoomed, drag to move around (Shift+drag to box-zoom further); press Esc to zoom out.

Each square is 2° × 2.5°, the climate model's own grid (roughly 250 km across at the equator), so a coastal city's square mixes land and ocean. Warming is measured from the 2015–2024 average and smoothed with a 10-year rolling mean. (NASA GISS-E2-1-G).
